The emergency calls we answer tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's warm air from the vents, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil frozen solid. And any time of year, it's breakers that trip every cycle, plugged condensate drains backing water into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have quit. Since our trucks carry capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, most of these are wrapped up in one visit, with the price agreed before any work begins.

A little caution before we arrive can head off a much larger repair. A frozen air conditioner should be shut off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. Running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us immediately.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Boston

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
