No Surprises

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Newton HVAC offers honest, upfront pricing across Newton, covering everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear quietly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, schedule an inspection sooner rather than later. Catching it early usually keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

Not every call is an emergency. Many Newton homeowners ask us to evaluate an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, a replacement on your own timeline beats a rushed swap in the middle of a heat wave. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your equipment has left.

Preventive Care

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

Preventing a breakdown beats paying for one. That's the idea behind our maintenance plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, check the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all tailored to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a regular maintenance schedule cuts operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Proper Sizing

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit turns on and off too often, wastes energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still falls behind on the hottest afternoons. We don't simply copy the tonnage of the old unit. We run a real load calculation using your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Homes change over time. A finished basement, an addition, or two new bedrooms can outgrow the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use much less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the honest pros and cons in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Common Problems

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Newton

Homes in Newton run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Newton HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A weak capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
